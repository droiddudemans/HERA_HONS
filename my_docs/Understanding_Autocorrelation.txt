----Autocorrelation----

--In words--
	"Antennas correlation with itself. It's basically the total power received power measured by a single antenna as a function of frequency and time (V_ii = <E_i E_i*>,
	a function of frequency). The E_i's are complex voltage measured by antenna i. <.> is time averaging."
--What for?--
	"Bandpass estimation"
	"Gain monitoring"
	"Detecting malfunctioning antennas"
	"RFI detection"
	"Absolute calibration"
	"Receiver noise estimation"
